The mod ecosystem

  1. Build it. A mod is a drop-in folder — code, its own storage, its own settings page.
  2. Submit it. Post your mod on the Nexus AI Discord server for consideration.
  3. Review. Submitted mods and their code are personally reviewed before anything is added.
  4. Registry. Mods that meet the requirements land in the Nexus AI Mod Registry, available to everyone. Ones that don't simply aren't added.
